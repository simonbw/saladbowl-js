How to play

SaladBowl is a party game that goes by many names, including Salad Bowl, Bowl of Nouns, Nouns and Proper Nouns, and The Nouns Game. You need at least 4 people, and everyone plays on their own phone.

Setting up

One person creates a new game and picks how many nouns each player writes and how long each turn lasts. Everyone else joins with the game code, or by tapping the code at the top of the screen to share a link or show a QR code.

Pick a name, then write your nouns: people, places, things, characters, anything everyone might know. They go into the bowl. Then pick a team, or tap Shuffle teams to split everyone up at random. When everyone has written their nouns and every team has at least 2 players, start the game.

Playing

Teams take turns. On your team's turn, one person gives clues: they see one slip at a time and try to get their team to guess it. Tap Correct when someone gets it, for a point. Tap Skip to draw a different slip; there's no penalty. Tapped Correct by mistake? Undo it.

Three rounds

Describe it. Say anything except the words on the slip. No gestures.
Charades. Act it out. No words, no sounds.
One word. You get one word per slip. No gestures.

A round ends when the bowl is empty. All the slips go back in for the next round. If the bowl empties partway through a turn, the clue-giver keeps going into the next round with the time they had left, plus a 5 second bonus.

The team with the most points after three rounds wins. Tap Play again to start a new game with the same teams.
