How to Play SaladBowl

SaladBowl is based off of a popular party game that goes by many names including Salad Bowl, Bowl of Nouns, Nouns and Proper Nouns, and The Nouns Game.

To play, you will need a group of at least 4 people, though larger groups are preferable. Everyone will need an internet-enabled device to play. On the main page, create a new game. You can choose how many nouns each player adds and how long each turn lasts.

Now everyone needs to join this game. They can do this by entering the game code on the homepage, by going to the game's link (tap "Invite players" to share it), or by scanning its QR code.

When you have joined a game, first enter your name. After this you will be asked to submit your nouns to the "bowl". Try to be creative.

Once you have entered your nouns, you will need to join a team. Join a team by clicking/tapping on it, or press "Shuffle teams" to split everyone up at random. When everyone is satisfied with the teams, press the "Start Game" button.

The game will have 3 rounds. During a round, teams take turns (60 seconds each, unless you picked something else) until the round is over. On a team's turn, one person will be the reader. The reader will randomly receive one noun at a time. The rest of the team will attempt to guess the noun the reader has. When a teammate guesses correctly, the reader presses the "CORRECT" button, and their team receives one point. If the reader doesn't like a noun, they can press the "SKIP" button to draw a new noun. There is no penalty for skipping. If the reader taps "CORRECT" by mistake, they can undo it.

During each round, the restrictions imposed on the reader vary. In the first round, the reader is allowed to speak any words that are not contained in the noun they are reading. No gestures are allowed. In the second round, the reader is not allowed to make any noise and must instead use actions to convey their message. In the final round the reader is only allowed to say one word per noun. Like in the first round, no gestures are allowed.

When all the nouns have been correctly guessed and the "bowl" is empty, the round is over. The nouns go back into the "bowl" and the next round begins. If the bowl empties partway through a turn, the reader keeps going into the next round with the time they had left, plus a 5 second bonus.

The team with the most points after all three rounds is the winner. Press "Play again" to start a new game. Everyone starts on their old team, and can switch or shuffle before starting.
